COVERAGE SIGN-OFF WORK (this campaign):
  1. The manual functional-coverage collector (env/ahb2apb_bridge_coverage.sv)
     was wired into the env and package and adapted to the reworked seq_item
     field names.  Baseline union coverage: 72.7% (93/128).
  2. Coverage exposed a real monitor bug: the AHB monitor called
     txn.post_randomize() after sampling the bus, and that seq_item's
     post_randomize() force-resets burst/size/trans_type to SINGLE/WORD/NONSEQ.
     It was overwriting the captured HBURST/HSIZE and hiding every burst/size
     bin.  Removing the call (a monitor must report what it observed) recovered
     the INCR4/HALFWORD bins -> 76.6% (98/128).
  3. The remaining 30 open bins were reachable AHB_TXN/ERROR stimulus holes
     (the suite only drove SINGLE/INCR4 bursts, WORD/HALFWORD sizes, and
     JUST_ABOVE/FAR_ABOVE write errors).  Added one directed test,
     test_coverage_closure_001, which drives every other HBURST encoding
     (INCR, WRAP4/8/16, INCR8/16) as write+read, a BYTE transfer, an APB_HIGH
     read, and the MID_RANGE / READ-direction decode errors -> 100% (128/128).

NOTE: The RTL (rtl_Design/ahb2apb_bridge.sv) was previously aligned to
ahb2apb_spec.pdf (CTRL/STATUS/ERROR_ADDR/ERROR_INFO layout, SOFT_RST, ENABLE
gating, 2^(TIMEOUT_VAL+4) timeout, W1C STATUS).  All 98 tests pass against it.
